# Shot list — close-ups aimed at the tables and panels (owner, with the phone)

Written 2026-10-21 after the panel-reading test (RESULTS 2026-10-21). That test
found the local model reads a panel from a close-up at 0.8 % word error against
5.7 % from the page photo (the same 11 panels), but **none of the six route tables has a close-up**,
so whether a close-up helps a table is untested. Every close-up then was also
shrunk by the old app; the app now uploads them at full size.

These shots also give the **fresh blocks** the mistake-flag work needs
(OPEN_PROBLEMS P2a): the 28 blocks of the first test are spent for choosing a
flag, because the ¾ → ¼ misreading was seen on them.

## What to shoot (the owner's via-ferrata guide)

For each item: one whole-spread photo as usual, then **one close-up per block,
with the block filling most of the frame**. Hold the phone square to the page
and keep the spine's shadow out of the frame where you can.

**The six tables (no close-up exists for any of them):**
1. "Tourenübersicht Klettersteige", group **F** (Sellagruppe/Langkofelgruppe)
   and group **I** (Latemartürme, Attilio Sieff) — same page.
2. Group **P** (Brentagruppe, P1–P14 and three "App" rows) and group **Q**
   (Q1–Q7, Rino Pisetta … Via dell' Amicizia) — same page.
3. Group **Q** continued (Gardaseeberge, Q8–Q19, Spigolo Bandiera).
4. The three-language glossary **Deutsch / English / Italiano** (Kuppe …
   Überschreitung). It is long: two close-ups (top half, bottom half) is fine.

**The panels with no usable close-up today:**
5. The glossary's first column page (Absatz … Gehzeit) — the column the
   current block box cuts in half.
6. The icon sidebar on the Popera page (printed page 42: "schwierig 1,7",
   "Sehr schön", coordinates, and the tiny walking-times lines at its foot —
   those lines are barely legible on every photo we have).
7. The "Rifugio Antonio Berti al Popera" hut box.

**New, for the flag test:** any **other** table or walking-times box in the
book with fractions in it (¼, ½, ¾ hours) — 5 to 10 of them. These must be
blocks the first test never read.

## What it decides

- Does a close-up help the model read a **table** the way it helps a panel?
  (Pre-register before reading, as for the panels.)
- Gives a fresh population for the number-specific mistake flag.

Nothing is cut or rendered from these shots until both are measured.
